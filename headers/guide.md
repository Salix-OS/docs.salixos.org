**Getting familiar with your new operating system.**

The Salix Startup Guide is meant to be read from start to finish. It takes you
from downloading and installing Salix to managing software, configuring your
system and finding your way around the command line. If you are new to Salix,
this is the best place to start.

Use the arrows at the top of each page to move to the previous or next
chapter. If you prefer to read the whole guide in one go, use the print button
on this page to view all chapters on a single page.
